SalesPadi brand kit (logo: "Coin P")
====================================

Colours
  Green        #0B8A4F   (main brand colour)
  Light green  #14A05E   (top of the icon gradient)
  Gold         #F5B83D   (the coin, use sparingly)
  Ink          #0F1724   (text on light backgrounds)
  Mint         #4FD394   (the word "padi" on dark backgrounds)
  Font         Geist, bold, written "salespadi" in lower case

Which file to use
  salespadi-app-icon-1024-square.png    Meta app icon, app stores, anywhere asking for a square image
  salespadi-icon-512.png / -192.png     Rounded icon with transparent corners: social profiles, slides, documents
  salespadi-apple-touch-icon-180.png    iPhone home-screen icon
  favicon.ico, favicon-16/32/48.png     Browser tab icon
  salespadi-logo-light-1320.png         Logo with name, on white backgrounds (letterheads, invoices, emails)
  salespadi-logo-dark-1320.png          Logo with name, on dark backgrounds
  salespadi-logo-transparent-1320.png   Logo with name, transparent, for light backgrounds
  salespadi-mark-green-512.png          The P on its own, for light backgrounds (stickers, merch, watermarks)
  salespadi-mark-white-512.png          The P on its own, for green or dark backgrounds

  The .svg files are the same designs as vectors: they stay sharp at any size, so give these to printers
  and designers. The logo SVGs use the Geist font for the name; if it isn't installed, use the PNGs instead.

Please don't
  stretch or squash the logo, change its colours, add shadows or outlines, or put the green icon on a
  busy photo. Leave clear space around it of at least a quarter of the icon's width.
